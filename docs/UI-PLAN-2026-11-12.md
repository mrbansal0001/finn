# Interface review and plan (12 Nov)

How this was done: every main screen rendered in Chrome at iPhone size (390×844, 2× pixels), at 320px, in dark mode and on a desktop. I tapped through cards, a ledger row, search and settings, and measured fonts, text sizes, tap targets, page heights and tap-to-page time. Scope: `web/src` only. No worker, database or data-shape changes.

## What to keep

- The identity: paper and ink, one blue accent, Newsreader serif headlines over Geist sans, thin rules instead of boxes, red and green only for direction. Dark mode is well balanced. No page scrolls sideways at 320px.
- The structure: a finite edition, then detail pages, then sources.
- Speed: a tap on a card opens it in about 0.2 s on the local server.

## What is wrong, in detail

### Typography

1. **Too many text styles.** I counted 23 combinations of family, size and weight, across 13 sizes from 10px to 28px. A tight scale needs about 6.
2. **Every number is in the monospace font.** That includes dates inside sentences ("Your edition since Wed 11 Nov · 12:07", "close Wed 11 Nov · 15:30"). Mid-sentence it reads like a typewriter and widens the line. Geist with tabular figures aligns numbers just as well and matches the text.
3. **Weak hierarchy in the feed.** Headlines are 17px serif against 16px body text, which is barely bigger.
4. **Small labels.** The uppercase labels ("MOVE · SBIN · WED 11 NOV") are 11px and appear about 650 times. Ledger column headers are 10px. Nothing should be under 12px on a phone.

### Edition (home)

5. **About 380px of header before any news.** The date in monospace, the "since" line, a two-line demo disclaimer on every visit, the window tabs, then the alerts box.
6. **The alerts box header wraps** on a phone, both "3 RULE MATCHES · AT THE / CLOSE" and "your rules: ±5% · 2× / volume ›". The up/down arrows are grey here but coloured everywhere else.
7. **The empty state is louder than the news.** "No lead story" is followed by a three-line, 28px serif paragraph.
8. **Feed cards carry full detail.** Each results card shows the whole table plus a four-line "checked: PBT − tax = PAT; half year = …" caption. Opening "worth a look" on the 7-day view makes the page 6,400px tall. The feed needs one summary line per card; the table belongs on the detail page.
9. **Small tap targets.** Only the headline text is a link (about 17–21px tall), not the card. Ticker links are 14–17px tall, "Filing ↗" chips 32px, "results PDF ↗" 16px.
10. **The network line wraps** to two or three lines under each feed card, and in compact rows it repeats what the detail page says.
11. **Mixed terms for the same thing:** "rule matches", "alerts", "worth a look", "updates worth reviewing".

### Card detail

12. **"‹ Edition" always returns to the 24-hour view**, so a visitor on 7 days loses their place.
13. **"Best placed: Farah Siddiqui…" repeats the first network line** directly below it.
14. **The "checked: …" caption is a wall of text.** It should be one line ("4 checks passed · matches exchange XBRL") that expands.

### Company page

15. **Order.** Ledger, people, results, then a long filings list. The investor's question ("what changed, what needs checking") comes late.
16. **Ledger legibility:**
    - column headers are 10px;
    - the legend wraps with a stray "·" at the start of a line;
    - row titles are 19px-tall buttons, so the row itself isn't the tap target;
    - status glyphs are 16px.
17. **Repeated network lines.** The filings list repeats a network line under every item ("Col. (retd.) Harish Menon…" six times on BEL).

### Person page

18. **Order.** The tie-strength arithmetic (0.37, 0.19, … = 0.89) comes before why this person matters right now.
19. **Draft wording.** The note template can read awkwardly: "Bharat Forge filed something I'm trying to read properly: Kalyani Strategic Systems unveils 120mm R.A.M.S."

### Search dock

20. **Height.** The dock is about 110px tall (search box plus a row with "Listen to brief" and "What counts") and covers about 13% of the screen on every page.
21. **Result order.** Search results list people before companies even when the query is a company. There are no counts and no "no results" row inside the panel.

### Motion and navigation

22. **No transitions anywhere.** Pages swap instantly and disclosures snap open (only the chevron rotates). The browser supports the View Transitions API, so a short cross-fade between pages and a gentle expand for disclosures would make it feel finished. Reduced-motion is already respected.
23. **Window tabs reload the page and jump to the top.** That's acceptable; the active pill could animate.

### Desktop

24. **A 448px column in a 1280px window.** That's fine for a mobile-first brief. Low priority: widen to about 560px on large screens.

## The plan (UI only, in order)

**Phase 1: foundations** (globals.css and the class names in components)
- A six-step type scale:
  - 28 serif: page title;
  - 22 serif: detail headline;
  - 19 serif: feed headline;
  - 16 sans: body;
  - 14 sans: secondary;
  - 12 sans: labels and metadata.
- Labels go to 12px; nothing under 12px.
- Geist with tabular figures replaces Geist Mono for every number. Mono is kept nowhere, or only in tables if it reads better there.
- A spacing scale (4/8/12/16/24/32) applied consistently.
- Every tappable thing at least 44px tall (or 24px with spacing for inline links).

**Phase 2: the edition**
- A compact header: date in the same font, the disclaimer once as a footnote, a single "since" line.
- A rebuilt alerts block: one-line title, rules link below, coloured arrows, company name with ticker.
- A quiet empty state (one line).
- Feed cards become summaries:
  - kicker;
  - 19px headline;
  - one line of key numbers ("Revenue +31.4% · PAT +63.9%");
  - one truncated network line;
  - the whole card tappable (stretched-link pattern; the person link stays separate).
- Wording unified: "Alerts", "Worth a look", "Routine, folded".

**Phase 3: detail pages**
- **Card:**
  - back link keeps the window;
  - "best placed" merged with the first network line;
  - checks collapsed to one line.
- **Company:**
  - order becomes header, then what changed (latest results summary and attention promises), then the ledger, then people (once), then filings without repeated network lines;
  - ledger: 12px headers, a legend that wraps cleanly, the whole row tappable, larger glyphs.
- **Person:**
  - "why now" and the draft first;
  - tie strength under a disclosure;
  - draft templates per event type, reading naturally.

**Phase 4: dock and motion**
- A slimmer dock: one row with the search box and a settings button; "Listen" moves to the edition header.
- Search results ordered by match type, with counts and a "no matches" row.
- A View Transitions cross-fade between pages (about 150 ms).
- Animated disclosures.
- The active window pill slides between tabs.
- All of it switched off under reduced motion.

**Phase 5: verify**
- Before/after screenshots at 320, 390, dark and desktop.
- Re-measure: number of text styles, text under 12px, tap targets under 44px, page heights.
- Lint and type checks; every card link still resolves.
- Then a real-phone check by you, since iOS Safari's keyboard and safe areas can't be tested from here.

## Not in this plan (needs backend changes; say if you want them)

- A browser for individual routine filings. Only category counts are exported today.
- A business description on company pages where the annual-report snapshot is missing.

## Result (implemented 12 Nov, same method of measurement)

| Measure (390px, all main screens) | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Distinct text styles | 23 | 16 |
| Text under 12px | 10–11px labels and headers (≈ 900 instances) | none, except decorative ▲▼ arrows hidden from screen readers |
| Tap targets under 44px (excluding inline links with an enlarged tap area) | 77 | ≈ 8 (source chips 40px, section shortcuts 40px) |
| 7-day edition height | 2,321px | 1,475px |
| Tap card → detail (local) | 214 ms, instant swap | 166 ms, 160 ms crossfade |
| Back from a card | always to 24 hours, top of page | the same window and scroll position |
| Card links checked | — | 157 local, 145 live, 0 broken |

Also done: the routine fold opens to each filing (worker exports `routine_items`); the dock is one row; search orders results by what was likely meant and matches word starts; a refused or mislabelled transcript no longer corrupts a ledger (TCS, ICICI Bank, Dr Reddy's, Sun Pharma rebuilt). Not done: a sliding pill between window tabs (the tabs are separate pages; they crossfade instead).
